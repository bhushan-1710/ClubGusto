## PART 17 — UI/UX DESIGN DIRECTION

- **Design style:** Warm editorial luxury — closer to a boutique hotel site than a generic banquet-hall template; avoid flat SaaS-card patterns.
- **Layout philosophy:** Full-bleed photography-led sections, generous whitespace between ornamental accents, content organized around decisions a family actually needs to make (capacity, price, amenities) rather than a generic "About/Services/Contact" template.
- **Grid:** Wide, cinematic image blocks for hero/property sections; a tighter, more structured grid for price/spec comparisons (Hall 1 vs Hall 2).
- **Typography:** Warm serif/slab-serif display + clean humanist sans body (see Part 4).
- **Color system:** Deep maroon/gold/ivory jewel-tone palette (visually-estimated — validate against real logo/brand file).
- **Buttons/cards:** Solid, confident buttons with a subtle gold accent rather than default rounded SaaS pill-buttons; avoid glassmorphism or gradient trends that clash with the "royal/heritage" positioning.
- **Navigation:** Simple, sticky top nav with a persistent "Enquire / WhatsApp" CTA — this is a high-intent, comparison-shopping category, so the path to contact should never be more than one tap away.
- **Image treatment:** Warm color grading consistent with the jewel-tone palette; avoid the cool, blue-white grading common to generic stock photography.
- **Motion:** Restrained — gentle fade/reveal on scroll for photography, no gimmicky parallax that would slow mobile load times (mobile responsiveness was explicitly flagged as a current weakness).
